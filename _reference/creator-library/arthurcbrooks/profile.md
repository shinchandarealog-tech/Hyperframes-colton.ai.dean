# @arthurcbrooks

**Platforms:** Instagram (@arthurcbrooks)
**Niche:** Happiness science / psychology / meaning and career, delivered as podcast and interview clips by Harvard professor Arthur C. Brooks.
**Why the user saves them:** It's the reference channel for the "premium minimal" educational editing style the user wants for client podcast/talking-head clips (see the `premium-minimal-edit` skill).

## Signature vibe
Calm, literary, expensive. White captions with one oversized hero word (bold sans for facts, serif italic for feelings) cut on the stressed word to classical paintings, cinematic b-roll and clean white/blue cards. One royal-blue accent, used only on white or as flat cards. Longer pieces add documentary devices (Polaroid collages, a life timeline, research papers with highlighter).
