# Auravedic: reference analysis (3 client-supplied reels)

Source: Google Drive files sent with the client brief (30s AI-made sample video for auravedic.com, 12% opacity watermark).
Method: every video sampled at 4 fps on timestamped contact sheets, plus ffmpeg scene-cut detection. All 3 are 9:16 at 30 fps.
Audio was **not** transcribed: the Whisper model download is blocked on this network. All three have continuous VO/music at about -17 LUFS.

All three are **fully or mostly AI-generated ads**. The client wants to see that same skill.

## What all 3 share (the "client style")

1. **AI-generated people and products**, photoreal, warm natural light, Indian women with long dark hair
2. **Hair/scalp problem → Auravedic product → ritual (pour/massage) → mechanism (scalp/strand macro) → glossy-hair result → packshot**
3. **Real product label is always legible** on the hero shots (they care about accurate packaging)
4. **Motion-blur transitions** (whip, zoom-blur, flash-white, particle dissolve), almost never a hard cut
5. Golden oil/serum as the visual hero material (pours, drops, ribbons, waves)
6. Fast first 3s hook; result shots near the end; product packshot as the last frame

## Recommended direction for our 30s sample

Closest fit is **REF A (cinematic) with one REF B ingredient-character beat**. Hit all of these in 30s:
- 0–3s: kinetic-type hook (problem word wall), in gold condensed type on plum
- 3–6s: AI problem shot (hair fall / dandruff) + 3-word particle-type sting
- 6–9s: product hero reveal with motion-blur push (real label, accurate)
- 9–13s: macro pour into palm → scalp massage
- 13–17s: ingredient beat (pop-in ingredient badges with gold rings, or 1–2 mascot cameos)
- 17–19s: scalp/strand mechanism macro (golden wave)
- 19–23s: glossy-hair result (hair flip, look-back)
- 23–27s: gold kinetic tagline, one word per beat
- 27–30s: packshot with gold liquid ribbon + "auravedic.com" + **12% opacity watermark throughout**

**Avoid:** AI-garbled label text on the packshot (REF A 31s). Composite the real label and product photo instead of letting the model redraw it.
