## Captions  ← THE KEY SECTION
**Overall idea:** "spatial kinetic typography". Every word is its **own object**, placed at its own position and rotation, appearing exactly when it's spoken. Words do NOT replace each other — they **pile up** into a scattered, curving phrase that wraps around the speaker, then the whole phrase clears at the end of the sentence (or a scene cut).

- **Style:** word-by-word, synced to the spoken word start (±1 frame). 2–8 words stay on screen at once.
- **Pop-in animation (per word):**
  1. **Letter-by-letter typewriter reveal**, very fast: ~1 frame per letter at 30fps ("h" → "ho" → "hol" → "hold"; "h" → "ha" → "hand"). A 4–5 letter word is fully on screen in ~0.13–0.2s.
  2. Each new letter comes in with a soft **blur/opacity fade (~2 frames)**, not a hard pop, so it reads as smooth and premium.
  3. There's no big scale-bounce on normal words. Emphasis words ("this", "PROOF", "EVIDENCE") arrive bigger and slightly faster, sometimes with a short slam/settle.
- **Layout ("the path"):** words follow a **curved path / spiral** around the speaker. Each new word steps down and to the right of the last, and the rotation keeps increasing along the path (0° → 15° → 30° → 90° vertical → 180° upside-down). In other sentences the path runs along an arc across the top ("Companies spend…" curls around the frame edge) or a staircase down the middle.
- **Depth:** some words sit **behind the speaker** (her head and hair occlude the "I'm") — this needs a subject cut-out/matte layer on top of the text.
- **Font system (3–4 roles, all mixed within one phrase):**
  | Role | Look | Used for | Color |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Base | rounded geometric sans, semibold, lowercase (Poppins / Quicksand / Comfortaa feel) | most words | white, soft dark drop-shadow + faint glow |
  | Emphasis | same family, **extra-bold/black**, a bit larger | key word of the phrase ("hand", "this", "logo", "properly", "consistently", "you") | **deep red** |
  | Whisper | small **handwritten/script italic** | filler words ("on", "that", "you", "actually", "shown") tucked between bigger words | white |
  | Punch | tall **condensed all-caps** (Bebas / Anton feel), sometimes faux-3D or stamped/distressed | the payoff noun ("TRUST", "PR" → "PROOF", "EVIDENCE", "REBRAND") | red with white edge, or white |
- **Active-word treatment:** no karaoke color-swap — the *newest* word is the active one. When an emphasis word lands, the older words sometimes **dim/shrink slightly** so the new word dominates.
- **Doodle accents:** hand-drawn white "!!!" strokes, a red arc swoosh around a word, red corner brackets (selection-box style) framing "logo", small burst/spark lines next to the "!!", typed "!!" after "do".
- **Position:** mid-frame, around the chest/head of the speaker — never a fixed bottom subtitle bar.
- **Size:** medium-large base words (~6–8% of frame height); punch words are huge (25–40% of frame height).

## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Spiral word-pile captions** — one absolutely-positioned `<span>` per word from `transcript.json`; each word gets an `x, y, rotation` from a path function (staircase / arc / spiral), a GSAP char-split typewriter reveal at the word's `start` (stagger ≈ 0.033s/char, each char `opacity 0→1`, `filter: blur(6px)→0`, `y: 6→0`), and the phrase clears together at the sentence end.
- **Four font roles** — tag each word as base / emphasis / whisper / punch (emphasis = the stressed noun or verb, whisper = filler words like on/that/you/the, punch = the payoff word). Map to CSS classes.
- **Text-behind-subject** — needs a matte of the speaker (a background-removed copy of the clip layered above the text). Only on the hook or hero moments; it's the "wow".
- **Grade punch** — `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.2)` on the video wrapper for 0.5–1s on the emphasis line.
- **Doodle accents** — SVG stroke-draw (`stroke-dashoffset`) for "!!!", arcs around words, and corner brackets; the `/hyperframes` skill's marker-sweep / hand-drawn circle patterns cover this.
- **Punch nouns** — condensed caps + red + quick slam (scale 1.4→1, 0.12s, `power4.out`) plus a tiny shake.
