## Pacing
Shots: **6** (one per section) · avg **4.1s** · min 2.26s (#3) / max 4.87s (#4) · face is on screen 100% of the time (no b-roll at all).
**The real pace is set by overlays, not cuts:** a new text or graphic element arrives about every **0.5–0.8s**, and a full "card" (icon row, mockup, chart) arrives about every 1.5–2s. Longest unchanged hold: "Performance marketing" pill + chart, 12.5–15.7s (3.2s).

## Cut logic
- Every cut is a **section change** (pain → insight → brand → services → price → CTA). Cuts never fall mid-thought.
- Each cut is also a **location change**: same outfit and day, a different spot on the same street, so it reads as "she's walking through town".
- 4 of 5 cuts are **covered by a light-leak flash** (3.93, 10.83, 15.7, 20.1); 8.57 is a hard cut. That's about 80% flash, 20% hard.

## Captions & typography
- **Keyword captions, not full subtitles.** Every spoken phrase appears, but as a **two-tier lockup**: one **KEYWORD** huge, plus 1–3 **connector words** small around it ("mein apna / **Dehradun**", "daal dene se / **Post** / kaam nahi chalta!!").
- Font: one family throughout. Heavy geometric sans, closest match **Poppins 700/800** (or Gilroy ExtraBold). Connector words in the same family at 500–600.
- Sizes (of 1280px height): keyword cap height about **5.5–7%** (≈70–90px font size); "Studio"/"Social Media" up to about **9%**; connectors about **2.2–2.8%** (≈30–36px).
- Case: Title/sentence case. Only the final CTA is ALL CAPS ("DM US RIGHT NOW").
- Colors: connectors pure white `#FFFFFF`, soft drop shadow. Keywords mostly **cyan/electric blue** `#0CB4FC`/`#24CCFC`, with occasional **blue→violet gradient** ("Social media" at 12s), **lime green** for "Business" and "Right content/ads" labels, and **peach→pink gradient** `#DC866C`→`#CC546C` reserved for the brand word "Studio".
- Position: keyword sits **upper third, y≈20–35%, often BEHIND the head** (person matte in front). Connectors and secondary lines sit at y≈45–55% across the chest.
- Entrance: keywords **slide in horizontally from off-frame** (≈7 frames, expo-out) or scale-pop; connectors **type on word by word** in sync with the VO. Exit: hard off on the next beat, or carried out by the flash.
- Timing tightness: each word appears on its spoken word (±2 frames).
