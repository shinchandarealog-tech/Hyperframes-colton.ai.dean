## Pacing
- Total scenes: ~17
- Average scene length: ~1.5s
- Shortest / longest: 0.5s (clock) / ~4s (talking-head runs)
- Distribution: **front-loaded**. Five B-roll beats land in the first 3.5s, then it settles into face-cam with only the captions changing. A second burst of graphics comes at 7s ("Everyone has an idea") and at 13–17s (the chat-UI mock).
- The pace slows for the explanation (8–11s, 17–20s) and speeds up on story beats.

## Captions
- Style: **two-tier phrase captions**. Small all-caps lead-in words (white, ~14px at 360w) are built up word by word, then the **keyword lands big** underneath.
- Keyword font: **red-orange (#E8552B) italic serif** (Playfair/Didone-like) for emotional words (SAY THAT, STOPPING THEM, Technical Skills, POINT, BUSINESS, Difficult, Technology, Dream Apps). It changes to **condensed bold sans** (white with an orange "ENT" split) for "ENTREPRENEURS", and to clean white sans on the "mature" beat.
- Active-word treatment: the keyword **blur-fades/types on letter by letter** ("Ap s", "Grow ng up"), and the lead-in line shrinks above it.
- Position: centre-low (55–60% down the frame), over the chest. Never covers the face.
- Size: lead-in small, keyword large (~3–4× the lead-in).

## Transitions
- Flavors: **hard cuts** (most of them); a **push-in zoom** on text into the cut (2.0–2.7s); a **light-leak/white flash** into the gradient-mesh scene (12.9s, where the orange streak sweeps in diagonally); **blur-to-sharp** on face cuts (8.0s and 11.5s frames are motion-blurred); and **card pop/scale** for the UI input.
- Rotation: cut → zoom → cut → flash → cut. Never the same fancy transition twice in a row.
- Signature: **hard cut onto a B-roll that literally shows the spoken noun** (years → clock, companies → logos, engineers → desks).

## Face treatment
- Fullscreen face-cam only (no split-screen in this video). Medium close-up, centred. Wide two-shots are used as "B-roll" from the same shoot.
- Grade: warm and natural, light teal background, skin kept true.
- A subtle punch-in between sentences (an alternating 100% / ~110% crop) hides jump cuts.

## Audio reactivity / SFX
- Dense SFX layer: **~40 broadband transients in 26s** (one every ~0.6s). Spectrogram: tall full-band spikes (to 17–18kHz) on almost every caption keyword and cut. These are short **swooshes/clicks/ticks** under the speech, not a music-driven pulse.
- Low-band hits (boom/sub) around 2.1s (logo wall), 2.7s (Software), 5.0s (ENTREPRENEURS), 13.3s (into the UI scene) and 14.3–14.7s (typing/click).
- No obvious music bed. Speech dominates, and the SFX sit underneath.
- Captions don't pulse to a beat. They're timed to the words.
