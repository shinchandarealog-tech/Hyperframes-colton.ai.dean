## Signature vibe
A clean, premium "studio broadcast" look. One presenter in a lit photo studio, teal key light against a warm floor, with giant 3D words written onto the wall *behind* her and every supporting line in a frosted-glass card anchored to her body. Medium pacing: long held takes with a new graphic beat every ~2s, and wide/medium shots alternating per sentence. The brand moment is the only designed transition.
