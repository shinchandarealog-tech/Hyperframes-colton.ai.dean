## Captions
- Style: **phrase chunks that build line by line**, not karaoke. Each phrase types/reveals word-by-word and stays on screen until the scene ends. A 2nd line appears beneath in a lighter weight.
- Every phrase gets a different typographic treatment. Five caption "containers" rotate:
  1. **White glass card** with orange "?" badge: black bold line 1, grey regular line 2 (the question).
  2. **Frosted dark-glass pill**: white bold line 1, white light line 2 (the problem).
  3. **Orange glowing wall text**: "كل معلومة" with the key word heavier, set high on the back wall in neon-orange with bloom (the turn).
  4. **Curved white text riding a circle edge**: text bends along a glass-lens arc in the bottom-right corner (secondary line).
  5. **Centre-chest white + cyan stacked**: white bold line + cyan heavy word underneath (the payoff).
- Font feel: rounded geometric Arabic sans (IBM Plex Sans Arabic / Tajawal / Alexandria feel), bold + regular weights. Latin: condensed heavy grotesk all-caps (DIN Condensed / Barlow Condensed feel).
- Active-word treatment: key word in a heavier weight or in an accent colour (cyan or orange); no scale pops.
- Position: always **anchored to the body**: waist height (y≈55–65%), wall height behind head (y≈30–45%), or the floor (y≈80%). Never in a generic bottom-third bar.
- Size: large to huge. The hero word is ~60–80% frame width; supporting lines are medium.

## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Behind-subject 3D type**: three stacked layers: `<video>` background → hero word → **subject matte** (the same video with a background-removed alpha, e.g. an RVM/rembg-generated `.webm` with alpha or a ProRes 4444 `.mov` matte) on top. Extrusion = layered `text-shadow` steps (1px offsets ×10 in a darker teal), plus a soft outer glow. Reveal = `clip-path: inset(0 0 0 100%)` → `inset(0)` (RTL, so wipe right→left) with `power2.out` over ~0.6s, timed to the spoken noun from `transcript.json`.
- **Glass caption containers**: `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(140%)`, `background: rgba(255,255,255,.12)` (dark pill) or `rgba(255,255,255,.92)` (white card), 1px `rgba(255,255,255,.5)` border, 28–36px radius, inner highlight via `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.6)`. Enter with `scale .6→1, y +40→0, back.out(1.6)`. A badge circle (orange "?") enters 0.15s before the card.
- **Two-weight line build**: line 1 bold (700–800), line 2 regular (400) at ~85% size and lower contrast. Each word staggers in by 0.08s (`opacity 0→1, y 12→0`). Keep earlier lines on screen; never replace text mid-scene.
- **Rotate caption containers per sentence** (card → pill → wall-neon → path-text → stacked). The variety does the work that cuts would normally do.
- **Neon wall sign**: orange text with stacked `text-shadow: 0 0 8px #F07A3C, 0 0 24px rgba(240,122,60,.6)`, `mix-blend-mode: screen`, placed at wall height above the head.
- **Path text on a circle**: SVG `<textPath>` on an arc that matches a frosted glass circle (`border-radius:50%` + backdrop blur). Animate `startOffset` to make it glide along the edge.
- **Brand swoosh frame**: a full-frame SVG path in orange with a 2px gradient gold stroke and blur glow. It reveals by morphing/translating in from bottom-right while the video stays visible through the curved window. Pair it with a letter-by-letter caps title.
- **Card-flip wipe**: a tilted peach rectangle (`rotate:-12deg`) sweeping bottom→top across the frame in ~0.35s to cover the cut. Or the `transitions-cover` registry block.
- **Shot grammar**: alternate WIDE ↔ MEDIUM every sentence (crop/scale a single 4K wide take if there's only one camera), with a slow `scale 1→1.12` push on each hold.
- **Callback outro**: reuse the opening 3D-wall-word treatment on the final key word, then pop a big white CTA on the floor plane.
