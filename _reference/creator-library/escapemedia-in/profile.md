# @escapemedia.in

**Platforms:** Instagram (@escapemedia.in)
**Niche:** Indian social-media agency that posts client case studies (schools, local businesses), presented on location at the client's site.
**Why the user saves them:** Reference for result-driven promo edits (big stat, proof footage, DM CTA), studied ahead of a real-estate video gig.

## Signature vibe
Bright, airy, on-location 9:16 edits with a full-body presenter and giant brand-red stat typography layered *behind* him. They mix editorial interludes (notebook paper, red marker circles, serif type) with fast graphic beats (exploding props, floating proof cards, 3D social icons).
