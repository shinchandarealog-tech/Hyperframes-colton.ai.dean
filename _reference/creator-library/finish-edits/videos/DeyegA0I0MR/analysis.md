- **0.75s:** The camera **push-zooms into one eye**. A **cyan horizontal light streak / lens flare** sweeps across, and "…N DOLLARS" in big serif slides past (cropped off the left edge, as if the camera is moving across the text).

The hook works because the first frame is **not the speaker**. It's a cinematic, symbolic visual (money in the eyes) that *illustrates the sentence*, and the caption is set in a premium serif instead of the usual bold sans.

Common rules across all of them:
- **Two-tier hierarchy every time:** a *small* lead-in line (filler words) plus a *huge* payoff word or phrase. The filler stays small and the keyword is giant.
- **Word-by-word build** synced to speech (each word appears as it's spoken, nothing pops early).
- **Position:** left third of the frame, or the opposite side from the speaker. **Never over the face.** Mid-height, not bottom subtitles.
- **Accent = warm.** An orange highlight box on one key word, and gold gradient on money and outcome words.
- Text **stays on screen and holds** after the line ends, until the cut.

## Transitions
- Mostly **clean hard cuts on the word**. The cut lands right as the next sentence begins.
- **Light-streak / lens-flare sweep** inside the opener (cyan).
- **Camera push-in / zoom** within scenes (opener eye zoom, chalkboard push and tilt). Movement lives *inside* the shot, not in the transition.
- **Blur dissolve** for the final exit.
- Variety comes from **changing camera angle and source** at each cut (wide → profile → close), not from flashy transitions.

## Face treatment
- Fullscreen 16:9 for every speaker shot. No bottom-half or PiP.
- **Multiple angles / punch-ins** of the same speaker: medium front, slight push-in, profile three-quarter, tight close. The cut alternates crops so it feels like a multi-cam shoot.
- Slow **digital zoom** within some shots.
- **Grading:** warm, cinematic, amber/tungsten with crushed blacks. Compared to the "Before", the After is **warmer, higher contrast, more saturated in oranges, and darker in the shadows**, so it reads as a richer "film look".
- Text is placed in negative space beside the speaker, and in two scenes the subject is **masked in front of the text** (text-behind-person).

## Audio reactivity
- No visible beat-pulsing.
- Every caption word and every insert is synced to the **spoken word**, not to music.
- Audio continues quietly under the end card (~-40dB), likely a soft music tail. A low music bed under the voice is likely but not clearly separable from the frames alone.

## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Two-tier kinetic captions:** small sans lead-in line plus giant condensed-sans keyword, built word by word from `transcript.json` timestamps. Map to `/hyperframes` caption authoring with per-word `data-start`, using a GSAP `from({y:20, opacity:0})` stagger per word. Don't use karaoke bottom subs.
- **Highlight-box word:** one word per sentence gets an orange (`#C8742E`) rounded box behind it, wiping in left to right (`scaleX` 0→1, `transform-origin:left`).
- **Gold-gradient money/outcome words:** `background: linear-gradient(...gold...)` with `background-clip:text` on numbers and outcome phrases.
- **Rotate the caption style per sentence:** serif, then condensed slam, then text-behind, then handwriting, then circled word. Never repeat the same style twice in a row.
- **Hand-drawn ellipse around the punchline word:** SVG path with `stroke-dashoffset` animated to 0 (draw-on) over ~0.4s, then the word appears inside.
- **Explainer insert for any numbers or math:** chalkboard/whiteboard scene with icons, plus SVG branch lines that draw on, plus labels in outlined boxes. Hold ~3–4s and add a slow camera push (`scale` 1→1.15 plus slight `rotation`) on the wrapper. `flowchart` / `data-chart` registry blocks are a starting point, re-skinned as chalk.
- **Illustrative b-roll inserts:** cut away from the speaker on concrete nouns (money, clients, struggle). Inserts need image/video assets (stock or AI-generated), which the user would supply or approve.
- **Fake multi-cam from one camera:** alternate crops (100% / 115% / 130% scale plus a horizontal offset) on the speaker wrapper at each cut so a single-angle recording feels multi-angle. Animate a wrapper `<div>`, never the `<video>` itself.
- **Warm film grade:** CSS `filter` on the video wrapper (contrast up, saturation up slightly, warm sepia tint) plus a vignette overlay to match the amber/crushed-black look.
- **Text placed opposite the face:** captions live in the empty third of the frame, never over the face.
- **Light-streak sweep in the hook:** a blurred cyan gradient bar crossing the frame; the `light-leak` registry block or a custom CSS gradient bar works.
- **Blur-out ending:** final 0.3s `filter: blur(0→20px)` before the end card.
