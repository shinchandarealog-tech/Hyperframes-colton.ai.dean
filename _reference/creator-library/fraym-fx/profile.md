# @fraym-fx

**Platforms:** Instagram (@fraym.fx)
**Niche:** Video editor for UGC / influencer brand ads — posts before/after edit showcases ("DM for such UGC Edits").
**Why the user saves them:** Reference for the *edited* look — how a raw talking-head becomes a polished, aesthetic UGC ad with B-roll inserts, layered captions, and product reveals.

## Signature vibe
Soft, girly "Pinterest journal" aesthetic: cream paper cards, scrapbook collages of tilted phone clips with stickers, polaroid frames with hand-drawn hearts, retro device frames. Captions pair clean white sans with thin calligraphy script for emphasis, plus acid-yellow inner-thought callouts and typewriter search-bar text. Hard cuts only — the motion lives inside the inserts.
