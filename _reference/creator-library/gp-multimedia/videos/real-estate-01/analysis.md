- 0.5: a cluster of **floating rounded image cards** (examples of agent reels: "Ladies", "Meet…", "28…") pops in around him in a loose 3D grid; some sit behind him.
- 2.0: "Waiting / to be / found" lands as a big stacked title **behind** him (he walks in front of it).

## Pacing
- One continuous walk-and-talk per location; **jump to a new location every sentence** (~3–6s each): pool day → pool night → balcony → grey house → white house → driveway.
- Scenes: ~9 locations / 32s ≈ 3.6s each, but a new graphic lands every ~1–2s, so it feels fast.
- Last 6s: static wide CTA shot, calmer.

## Captions

  1. **Tiny word-by-word subtitle:** one word at a time, plain white sans (SF Pro / Inter feel), small (~2.5% of height), sitting at the speaker's **waist/chest, centered on him**. Quiet, almost a guide track.
  2. **Big keyword titles behind the speaker** (rotoscoped, he occludes them) on 1–3 key phrases per sentence.
- Keyword title looks (they change per beat):
  - "Waiting to be found": bold geometric sans, steel-blue → white gradient, stacked and left-aligned, letters fade up.
  - "ATTENTION": tall condensed caps, **cyan neon glow** (night scene), then pixel-glitches out.
  - "Everybody else … the same": white bold sans, three sizes.
  - "High-end real estate": white, semi-transparent, two lines.
  - "But that shift / **ISN'T**": white sans on top + huge **red glowing** condensed caps.
  - "GP Multimedia": giant serif logotype with a blur zoom.
  - "IMPOSSIBLE" (white condensed) over "**IGNORE**" (huge red condensed, behind).
  - "STAND OUT / online": condensed caps with a grey/white gradient, white sans "online".
  - "DM me / **Video**": white sans + blue gradient condensed.
