## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Bar-placeholder reveal**: a `div` with `background:#fff` (or black on paper) at the exact word width shows for 2–3 frames, then swaps to the text via `tl.set`. It's cheap, very legible, and frame-precise. Build it as a reusable caption-entry helper.
- **Three-world rhythm**: author 3 background sub-compositions (face-cam, `paper` = #e4e4e4 + graph-paper SVG pattern + grain + scratch lines, `void` = #0a0a0a + grain) and hard-cut between them. Content nouns go to paper/void and connective speech stays on the face.
- **Spatial sentence around an image card**: a central portrait card (B&W photo or video, ~35% band height), with each new word pinned to a corner/side of the card as it's spoken. Map it to a custom sub-comp. The `app-showcase` layout logic is a close starting point.
- **Pixel-stepped circle mask + rapid image cycle** (swap every 3 frames) for a "concept" noun. Use `clip-path: polygon()` with stair-stepped points, or the `grid-pixelate-wipe` component for the in/out.
- **Red-flash emphasis**: on the stressed syllable, flip the word `color` to the accent for 2–4 frames, then back to white (no scale pop). Optionally jolt the face wrapper (scale 1.08 + y offset for 3 frames).
- **Typewriter with a red `>` caret** for a single key noun ("brain"), then burst a collage out from it.
- **Dot-matrix dissolve** as the face → paper bridge: seeded-PRNG square dots on a grid, opacity stepping in clusters.
- **Captions in the speaker's negative space**, not lower-third. With a 9:16 build this maps to placing words beside or above the face rather than at the bottom.
