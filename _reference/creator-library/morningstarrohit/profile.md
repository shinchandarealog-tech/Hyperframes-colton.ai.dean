# @morningstarrohit

**Platforms:** YouTube — "Morning Star Rohit" (@Morningstarrohit, ~32.5k subs at time of study)
**Niche:** Futures prop-firm reviews and comparisons (Tradeify, FundedNext, etc.), personal funded-trader journey.
**Why the user saves them:** Style reference supplied by a client for a 90-second sample edit — the client wants their long-form YouTube video edited in this style.

## Signature vibe
Premium "dark study" talking head (warm lanterns, two cameras, punch-ins) intercut with a clean, consistent indigo-gradient graphics world: glowing glass stat cards, circular logo badges, 3D podium reveals. Opens with a monochrome "COMING UP" teaser of the payoff. Calm, conversational pacing — graphics land on every number and firm name rather than on a beat.

## Full-video format (from the 24-min study)
B&W "COMING UP" teaser → disclaimer → dense indigo-graphics intro (~1:30) → long body that alternates front talking head, screen recordings framed with a face-cam PiP, and an overhead whiteboard with PiP. Recurring "use code" lower banner, a social-handles bar, small DISCLAIMER pill. No captions, no SFX, no end screen.
