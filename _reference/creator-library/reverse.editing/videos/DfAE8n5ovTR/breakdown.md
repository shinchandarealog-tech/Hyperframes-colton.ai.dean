## 4. Getting a clean take from under the graphics

**From this clip: you can't, at least not cleanly.** Graphics cover the subject in almost every frame of S1, S3, S5 and S6, and S2/S4 are AI stills with the text baked in. The nearest clean frames are 0.00, around 5.25 (partial) and 13.3–13.6 (labels in the background only). Inpainting the rest would mean rebuilding someone else's footage, and the result would look smeared. Use the clip as a timing reference only.

**For your version, shoot it so the clean take always exists:**
1. **Record the talking head on its own** (locked-off tripod, same framing for the whole take, 4K if possible so you have room for the 10% S6 punch-in and the 8% S3 widen). Leave about 1 s of still, silent room before and after.
2. **Keep graphics on separate tracks.** In Hyperframes the `<video>` sits on `data-track-index="0"` and every graphic is a `.clip` on track 1+. Turning the graphics off, or rendering only the video track, gives you the clean take at any time.
3. **"Text behind subject" (G1):** don't key it in-camera. Shoot against a background that separates well from you, then make a matte (Runway/CapCut "remove background" export as a PNG sequence or alpha `.mov`). Layer it as video → giant word → matted-you → front graphics.
4. **Clean plate:** after the take, step out of frame and record 3 s of the empty set. That gives you a background plate for any patch-up.
5. **Audio:** record voice on a lav/shotgun into the camera or a separate recorder. Keep it untouched (your speech timing is the master clock) and add SFX as separate `<audio>` elements.
