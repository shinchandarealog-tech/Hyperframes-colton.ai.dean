# @tailoredfit

**Platforms:** Instagram Reels (handle `tailoredfit`, from the uploaded file names); likely cross-posted to TikTok/Shorts
**Niche:** Physio-style fitness: joint pain, mobility and strength (ankles, knees, tendons) taught through short exercise programs that funnel to their app
**Why the user saves them:** The client supplied these as the editing style they want for their own workout videos.

## Signature vibe
A clean, bright, cinema-camera gym look, explained like a physio and edited like a premium reel. Bold word-highlight captions sit in the negative space, glowing anatomy overlays and neon form cues are drawn right onto the body, a white infographic card appears mid-video, and each video ends with a self-aware "you'll save this and forget" skit before a comment-keyword CTA.
