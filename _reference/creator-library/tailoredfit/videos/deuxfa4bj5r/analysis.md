## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Body-part anatomy overlays**: PNG/SVG muscle or bone art with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, a red/orange glow and an opacity pulse, pinned over the joint. Build as a `.clip` div layered over the `<video>` wrapper
- **Neon form cues**: SVG arrows, angle lines and rep numerals drawn on with `stroke-dashoffset`, with a glow filter (`drop-shadow(0 0 8px red)`)
- **Word-highlight phrase captions**: 1–4 word chunks, heavy rounded sans, one active word recolored. Change the accent color per section
- **Perspective floor text**: CSS `transform: perspective(800px) rotateX(55deg) rotateZ(-8deg)` so captions lie on the floor or bench
- **3-row split-screen preview** with "1." "2." "3." badges in the hook
- **White explainer card**: grid-paper bg, condensed black headline ("ONE MOVE."), red labels, dashed-border video tiles, step-by-step build, exits with a 3D card flip
- **PiP rounded context tiles** (sprinter, ankle brace) next to the subject
- **Phone-mockup CTA** with a fixed "COMMENT 'KEYWORD'" headline plus running captions
