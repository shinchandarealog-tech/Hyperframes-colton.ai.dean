# @themilesholden

**Platforms:** Instagram Reels (all 8 studied files are IG reel downloads)
**Niche:** AI-generated "spec ad" brand films. He shows that a rough hand sketch can be turned into a polished, cinematic app commercial with an AI model ("Opus 5.5"), and funnels viewers into a comment-for-guide lead magnet.
**Why the user saves them:** A highly repeatable, faceless short-form format: a fixed before/after split-screen shell, premium motion-design storytelling in about 15s, and a built-in engagement CTA. It's a strong template for showcase content.

## Signature vibe
A dark, minimal stage with two 9:16 panels: a black-ink sketch on paper on the left and a lush, brand-coloured, continuously moving render on the right. Each video is a 6-act mini brand film for a familiar app (tap icon → transform → real use case → payoff → word-stack → slogan slam → logo), with no hard cuts, no face and no spoken captions. The motion energy changes to fit each brand, but the chrome never changes.
