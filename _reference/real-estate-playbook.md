# Real-Estate Reel Playbook (distilled from @escapemedia.in + @fobet.media)

Neither reference is a property video. Both are **agency "client results" ads**. Their structure is the same funnel a real-estate reel needs: **result/hook → proof → offer → DM CTA**. This file translates their techniques to property content. Per-video detail is in `creator-library/*/videos/*/analysis.md`.

## The shared skeleton (both videos)
| Beat | escapemedia.in (16.7s, 9:16) | fobet.media (42s, 16:9) | Real-estate version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook 0–2s | Giant red "60%" behind the presenter + exploding forms | "00:30 video → ₹5,00,000 client" + flying cash | "₹85L → 3BHK with lake view" / "SOLD in 7 days" slammed behind the agent at the property gate |
| Context | "in just 3 months", shown on a TV in the principal's office | Client cut-out + "3 MONTHS" + reel grid | Location pin, distance chips (Metro 2 km, School 1 km) |

| Offer | "2X" | ₹4,00,000 on a money wall + highlighted contract | Price on a frosted card + highlighted RERA no. / payment plan |
| CTA | "DM us today / BOOK your slots" + 3D social icons | "Comment & DM me "GROW"" glass box | "Comment "VISIT" for the brochure" / "Book a site visit" |

## Top techniques to build in Hyperframes
1. **Depth-sandwich stat:** plate → giant number → cut-out agent → captions (escapemedia). Needs a person matte (alpha `.webm` or segmented clip).
2. **Two-tier caption lockup:** small setup words + one giant condensed keyword, in negative space beside the face (fobet).
3. **Burst props toward camera:** keys, floor plans, "SOLD" tags (escapemedia's application forms).
4. **Scene-shrinks-into-a-screen:** the walkthrough collapses into a phone held by a buyer (escapemedia's TV).
5. **Document highlight reveal:** blurred-copy background, slow push-in, yellow highlighter sweep over price / RERA / possession date (fobet).
6. **Frosted-glass price card over a textured wall** with a rising line chart for appreciation (fobet).
7. **Floating rounded proof cards** of rooms or amenities behind the presenter (escapemedia).
8. **Notebook explainer with an SVG marker ellipse** for "3 reasons" (escapemedia).
9. **Colour-flash / streak-tunnel transitions** as the glue (fobet). Never repeat the same transition twice in a row (both).
10. **Keyword-DM CTA** with a glass box or 3D social icons (both).

## Pacing targets
- 9:16, 15–30s. A graphic beat every 0.5–0.8s, with a scene change every ~2.5–3.5s.
- One calm "breather" beat (notebook / testimonial) before the offer and CTA.

## Palette starting points (override with brand-tokens)
- Premium/luxury: navy `#0E1A2B` + cyan `#2BB5E8` + white (fobet-style glass UI)
- Bold/local: brand red `#D0121B` + white + environment colour (escapemedia-style)
- Money/contract highlight: `#F5C518`
