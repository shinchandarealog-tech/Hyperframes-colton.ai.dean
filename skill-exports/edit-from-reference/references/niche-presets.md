## How to adapt any preset

1. Swap in the client's **brand accent** first; keep everything else.
2. If the client's footage is warm or cool, nudge the light base toward it so cards feel related to the footage.
3. Check contrast: caption text needs ≥4.5:1 against its background (≥3:1 for hero words over ~100px).
4. One preset per video. Don't mix two cutaway languages unless the topic changes.
