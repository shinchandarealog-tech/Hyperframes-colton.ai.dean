# Style fingerprint: premium minimal (@arthurcbrooks reference)

Distilled from 4 real reels studied frame by frame at 2fps with word-level transcripts (17.8s, 37.9s, 72.6s and 150s long), plus the tutorial's 10 principles. Where the tutorial and the real channel disagree, **the real channel wins**.

## What the tutorial says vs. what the channel actually does

| Tutorial emphasis | Real channel |
|---|---|
| Lots of paper-and-illustration animated scenes (blue cloth-textured figures, line + illustration scenes) | **Mostly talking head + fast cutaways** (paintings, film b-roll, cards). The illustrated scenes are occasional accents. |
| Blue cinematic text | Blue text **only on white/paper or flat blue cards**. Over footage, captions are always white. |
| Paper texture everywhere | Paper grain on white canvases; footage keeps its own look with light film grain. |

## Signature moves (consistent across all reels)

- **Hook:** a visual within the first second. Either open straight on a painting or b-roll carrying the thesis word ("You need to be **bored.**" over a Van Gogh self-portrait), or show the face for ~0.5s and cut to art on the first loaded noun. Never a title card.
- **Caption system:** white, center or lower-middle over the chest; one hero word per phrase at 2–3× size; satellites ≈20–30% of hero size, tucked tight; **font by meaning** (bold sans for facts, serif italic for feelings); words build one at a time in fixed positions; no stroke, pill or box.
- **Color rule:** text is white on footage and blue on white. Blue is a saturated royal or electric blue (`#1F4FD8`–`#0A47FF`).
- **Cut rhythm:** hard cuts on the stressed word; a cutaway every 1.5–3s; the face is never held more than ~6s.
- **Face:** fullscreen 9:16 only, with 2–3 crops (wide/medium/tight) rotated like a multi-cam shoot. Never bottom-half or picture-in-picture.
- **Energy:** breathing. Faster around lists and claims, slower on emotional lines, a clean ending.
- **Endings:** a blue-on-white summary card ("That's how to *think* about it."), a painting bookend, or the face with a serif "Thank you".

## Script → scene table (observed)

| Script moment | What the edit shows |
|---|---|
| Thesis / loaded abstract noun ("masters", "bored", "waste", "balance") | Painting (classical or classical-style) that literally depicts it, plus a giant hero word |
| Concrete scenario ("woman with a mug", "running", "gym") | Cinematic b-roll, often black-and-white with grain |
| List of 3 ("wonderful, beautiful, generative") | One cutaway per item, 0.5–1s each; or a kaleidoscope shard list |
| A number, growth, "progress" | White paper graph, blue line climbing out of frame |
| "Studies show" / an experiment | Flat blue claim card, then the research paper with a yellow highlighter sweep |
| A named person | Name callout: photo, curved line, name on a blue box |
| Speaker's own history | Polaroid collage on white plus a life timeline with a blue marker |
| A question the audience asks | Recreated phone UI (a DM bubble typing out) |
| A quote / turning point | Blue radial card with big quotation marks |
| Pause after a big claim | Breath card: black or white, ONE serif word, 0.5–1s |
| A lesson ("this is your teacher") | Rounded inset b-roll on black |
| The speaker addresses "you" | Reverse shot of the interviewer or audience |

## Don't copy

- Copyrighted film clips the channel uses (Hollywood movie scenes). For client work use licensed stock, public-domain paintings, or the client's own footage.
- The channel's personal photos, books, institutions, social UI and catchphrases ("Hi friends"). Use the client's own.
- The shoot look (black studio, moody atrium) comes from the footage, not the edit.
