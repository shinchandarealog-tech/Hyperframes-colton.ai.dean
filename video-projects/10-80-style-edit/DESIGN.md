# DESIGN: 10-80-style-edit

Derived from `_reference/creator-library/daniel-hee/videos/DdreUxnSdt3/analysis.md` (the user asked for "edit exactly like the reference").

## Style Prompt
Calm, premium talking-head. Fullscreen face-cam with jump-cut punch-ins. Small white 1–3 word phrase captions sit just under the chin. One key word per sentence is promoted to a huge orange-gradient heavy-italic caps word with a strong orange glow that spills onto the footage. Product specs get explained once or twice as dark, warm-glowing minimal capsule-bar diagrams that build on the spoken words, with hand-drawn white arrows and orange labels. Graphic → face exits with an orange light leak.

## Colors
- `#FF7A1A` primary orange: keyword fill, bar fill, glow
- `#FFC066` amber/gold: keyword gradient top, flare highlight
- `#120806` warm near-black: diagram background edges
- `#6B2A10` ember: diagram background center glow
- `#FFFFFF` white: small captions, arrows, ticks

## Typography
- Small captions: Inter 600, ~50px, white, soft dark shadow, no stroke
- Keywords: Montserrat 900 italic, uppercase, 100–130px, gradient fill + glow (upright variant for some lines)

## What NOT to Do
- No karaoke word highlighting, no caption pills/boxes (except the single outlined-pill accent)
- No second accent color; everything is orange/white
- No whip-pans, glitches or zoom-blur transitions
- Don't cut or retime the speaker's audio
- Don't copy the reference's ad wrapper (grey canvas, Raw card, "$70 Ads", "DM me ADS")
