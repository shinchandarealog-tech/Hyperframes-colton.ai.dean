# Auravedic Hair Growth Serum: "It starts at the root"

## Style Prompt
Warm amber luxury. Dark espresso shadows, honey-gold liquid light, cream type. Premium Ayurveda, not clinical, not cartoon. Problem beats use cream/white type; solution beats use gold, so the colour itself tells the story. Every cut is masked by motion: whip-blur, zoom-blur or a gold flash, never a hard cut.

## Colors
- `#1a0f08` espresso: base canvas / type-card background
- `#7a4a1e` amber: glow, gradients
- `#e8b54a` liquid gold: solution type, rings, CTA
- `#f3e6cf` cream: problem type, secondary text
- `#fff4dc` highlight: gold-gradient top stop, flashes

## Typography
- **Bebas Neue**: display caps (hook, THIN/WEAK/FALLING, taglines)
- **Playfair Display** (italic): ingredient names, URL
- **Inter**: small labels, CTA pill

## What NOT to Do
- No hard cuts
- No gold on problem beats (gold = Auravedic's solution)
- No text over the product label
- No text below y=1650 (Reels UI zone)
- No medical claims beyond "Stronger roots. Fuller hair."
