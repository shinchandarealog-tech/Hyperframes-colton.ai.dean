# DESIGN — Bauhaus "Strip It Down"

## Style Prompt
The video begins ornate (sepia, gilded baroque frame, damask wallpaper, blackletter and serif type, slow floaty motion) and gets stripped into Bauhaus (flat cream paper, hairline grid, primary geometric shapes, heavy geometric sans, mechanical snap motion). The edit acts out the script: decoration out, function in.

## Colors
- Cream paper `#F2EBDD` (Bauhaus canvas)
- Black `#111111` (structure, type, borders)
- Red `#E1251B` = what got removed (decoration, craft)
- Yellow `#F5C400` = impact / today (also the caption label)
- Blue `#1F4E9C` = function / the rule
- Act 1 only: antique gold `#D9B45A`, oxblood `#5A1414`, sepia grade

## Typography
- Bauhaus: Jost 700/900, uppercase, tight tracking
- Act 1 only: UnifrakturMaguntia (blackletter), Playfair Display italic

## What NOT to Do
- No gradients, glows or chrome on Bauhaus beats: flat colour only
- No gold or serif after the strip (beat 8), except the single "DETAIL" ornament that gets launched off
- Captions are one style the whole video (black on a yellow label), fixed position below the chin
- No text below y=1250 (Reels UI zone)
