# DESIGN — Bauhaus → Modernism

## Style Prompt
@edita.visuals-style talking-head edit about the Bauhaus. Face-cam is home base, interrupted every 2–4s by clean cream-canvas graphic scenes built from Bauhaus geometry (circle, square, triangle). Captions use a two-tier lead-in + keyword system. One accent colour (Bauhaus red) carries every keyword; yellow and blue appear only inside the Bauhaus shape motifs. Rounded cards, soft shadows, a sound effect on every visual event.

## Colors
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Accent (keywords, ✕, line, strike) | `#E3342F` Bauhaus red |
| Canvas | `#F3EDE2` warm cream |
| Ink | `#141414` |
| Motif only | `#F2B705` yellow, `#1F4FA3` blue |

## Typography
- Display / keywords: **Jost** 700–800 (geometric, Futura-like, the Bauhaus face)
- Emotional keywords: **Instrument Serif** italic
- Lead-ins & labels: **Inter** 500–600, uppercase, tracked
- Data labels: **JetBrains Mono**

## What NOT to Do
- No flat white captions without hierarchy; every caption has a lead and a keyword
- No new accent colours; yellow and blue only inside shape motifs
- No captions over the face (keep them at 55–65% of frame height)
- No two identical transitions in a row
- No dead air: the edit is already tight, don't pad it
