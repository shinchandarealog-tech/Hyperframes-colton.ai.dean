# AI shots for the hybrid Blinkit ad

Three realistic shots come from an AI video tool. Everything else (phone, app, cart, map, slogan type, logo) stays in the HyperFrames 3D build.

## Where each shot goes

| Time in ad | Source | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0s | **AI shot 1** | Fridge opens, empty milk carton ("7:50 PM" overlay added by me) |
| 2.0–10.0s | Code 3D | Phone tap → app search → cart → map ride |
| 10.0–11.6s | **AI shot 2** | Front door opens, rider hands over the yellow bag ("7:59 PM" overlay added by me) |
| 11.6–14.6s | **AI shot 3** | Groceries floating on yellow, behind the word stack and the "Everything in 10 minutes!" slogan |
| 14.6–17.0s | Code 3D | Glossy Blinkit icon + "Download now" |

## How to make each shot (works in any tool)

1. **Make the first frame as an image** (Midjourney, Nano Banana / Gemini, ChatGPT images, Flux). Use the *Image prompt*. Make 3–4 and pick the best.
2. **Animate it with image-to-video** (Kling, Veo, Runway, Hailuo, Sora). Upload the image as the **first frame** and paste the *Motion prompt*.
3. Settings: **9:16 vertical**, highest resolution available (1080×1920 ideal), **5 seconds**, highest quality mode. Turn off generated audio if offered; I'll add the sound.
4. Make 2–3 versions of each and send me your favourite (or all of them). Name them `ai1-fridge.mp4`, `ai2-door.mp4`, `ai3-groceries.mp4`.

**Rules for every shot:** no text, no logos, no brand names in the image. AI tools garble lettering, so I add all text and the Blinkit wordmark on top in the edit. Keep the camera smooth: no shake, no fast cuts inside the clip.

**Tool tips**
- **Midjourney:** add `--ar 9:16 --style raw --v 7` to the image prompt.
- **Kling:** use Image-to-Video, Professional/Master mode, 5s, relevance around 0.6.
- **Veo / Gemini:** start the motion prompt with "Vertical 9:16 video." and say "no audio".
- **Runway:** use Gen-4 with the image as the first frame, 5s.

## AI shot 1: The 7:50 panic (fridge)

**Image prompt**
> Vertical 9:16 photograph from inside an open modern refrigerator at night in an Indian apartment kitchen, looking at the shelves. Cool white fridge light, clean glass shelves, a jar with a yellow lid, a small glass jar with a green lid, a block of butter and two eggs in a tray. On the middle shelf, an empty milk carton lies tipped over on its side with the cap off and a small drop of spilled milk on the glass. A small blank yellow sticky note is stuck on the back wall above the carton. Shallow depth of field, focus on the empty carton. Premium photoreal commercial photography, soft cinematic lighting, cool blue-white tones. No text, no logos, no people.

**Motion prompt**
> The refrigerator door swings open toward the camera and sweeps out of frame on the left in the first second, cool light spilling over the shelves. Then a slow, smooth dolly-in toward the tipped-over empty milk carton on the middle shelf, which rocks slightly and settles. Gentle cold haze. Realistic physics, steady camera, no shake, no text, no logos.

**What I need from it:** a clear empty carton in the middle of the frame by the end. I use about 2 seconds of it.

A carton of milk, a bag of potato chips, a clear bag of ice cubes, a small chocolate cake with a candle, a pack of cookies and a few fresh limes float in mid-air around the edges of the frame, slightly rotated, with the centre of the frame left completely empty. Soft studio lighting, soft realistic shadows, glossy highlights, premium photoreal product commercial. No text, no logos, no brand names on the packaging.

**Motion prompt**
> The groceries burst outward from the centre in slow motion, then keep floating and slowly rotating around the edges of the frame while the camera gently pushes in. The centre of the frame stays empty. Smooth, weightless motion, steady camera, no text, no logos.

**What I need from it:** the centre of the frame kept clear. Words and the slogan go there, and I'll blur the first half behind the word stack.
