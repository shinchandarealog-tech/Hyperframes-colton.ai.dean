# Buddham Guest House — Reel Ad (DESIGN)

## Style Prompt
Warm, welcoming local-business reel for Buddham Guest House, Phulwari Sharif, Patna. Real walkthrough
footage carries the story; branded motion layers sit on top. Palette comes straight from the client
logo: deep navy for trust, saffron/gold for warmth ("sun halo" light), leaf green as a calm accent,
cream for the outro. Fast, punchy cuts synced to the Hindi voiceover, whip/zoom transitions with a
saffron light-leak on section changes, then a calm logo outro that holds.

## Colors
- `#14215E` navy — panels, caption plate, primary brand
- `#F28C1B` saffron — highlighted words, accents, pin icon
- `#F7B731` gold — halo glow, light leaks, thin rules
- `#2E7D32` leaf green — "GUEST HOUSE" / secondary accent (sparingly)
- `#FFF6E6` cream — outro canvas, text on navy

## Typography
- **Baloo 2** (600/800) — all Hindi (Devanagari) and display text
- **Poppins** (600/800) — Latin numbers / phone / English tagline

## What NOT to Do
- No hard cuts — every shot change is a whip, zoom-punch, or light-leak
- No burned-in third-party overlays (source clips were cropped/cleaned)
- No cold blues/purples outside the logo navy
- Don't let text cover the main subject of a room shot — captions live in the lower plate
- Phone number must always read exactly `790-310-2149`
