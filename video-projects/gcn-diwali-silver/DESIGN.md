# GCN Silver Merchant — Diwali Special · DESIGN

## Style Prompt
Festive-luxury silver showcase for Instagram Reels. Warm-black canvas, the product is lit (not colored):
silver glints, soft gold diya-light, slow push-ins on every shot. Calm, premium pacing driven by a Hindi
voiceover — real shop footage first, AI-generated lifestyle/idol shots only where real footage doesn't exist.
Transitions are soft and luminous (blur crossfade + gold light-leak flash), never hard cuts.

## Colors
- `#0b0a08` warm black — canvas, vignette
- `#e9e4da` silver-ivory — headline text (silver gradient)
- `#d4a94f` diya gold — accent lines, key words, logo (matches the GCN logo gold)
- `#ffd98a` flame glow — light-leak / halo only

## Typography
- Hindi key phrases: **Tiro Devanagari Hindi** (display serif) — weight 400
- Latin brand / address / phone: **Cinzel** (caps) + **Poppins** for small info text

## Logo
`assets/logo.png` (transparent, from the client's PNG). Small, top-right, on every frame.

## What NOT to Do
- No hard cuts, no glitch/RGB/tech transitions — this is devotional/luxury
- Never double text: when an AI clip already carries baked-in Hindi text, add no overlay text
- No flat white text — silver gradient + soft halo
- No more than 4 hues
- Never show the Gemini watermark (AI clips are cropped at prep time)
