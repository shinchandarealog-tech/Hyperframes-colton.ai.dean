## Differences from the reference, and why
1. **Caption colours on the speaker.** The reference speaker wears black, so white text works. Your client wears white, so white text vanished. Speaker pages use the reference's own light-ground inks: charcoal #3e3e3e (from its light b-roll pages) and rust #8f2e1e. Pills that were cream are rust, and gradient words have a soft shadow.
2. **Text zone.** The reference puts captions on the speaker's chest. Here captions sit below her chin in every frame (checked frame by frame), inside x 240–790 so they stay on her top and off her arms/hair. Some long lines are scaled down to fit.
3. **IG profile card (T01, T20)** uses placeholders: `client.handle`, `CLIENT NAME`, "CN" avatar, no stats. **I need her handle, name, follower numbers, a profile photo, and whether she is verified** (the blue tick stays only if she is).
4. **B-roll** is free Mixkit stock (licences in `broll/LICENSES.txt`), chosen to match her words (motherhood, career, faith, support). The reference uses product cut-outs, so swap in her own photos/clips if she has them.
5. **Rough cut.** Removed "I'd say" at the start, "you know what" and "you know," and shortened every pause > 0.16 s to 0.10 s → 32.4 s of speech. Templates use this tightened timing.
6. **Resolution.** The source is 1440×2560; templates are at the reference's 1080×1920. The final edit can be delivered at 1440×2560.
7. **Sound analysis.** Demucs couldn't be installed here (blocked download). The reference's SFX were found by onset detection instead. It has a music bed and very few hits. This only matters for the full edit.
