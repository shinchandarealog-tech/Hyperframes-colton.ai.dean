# Janhit Herbal Care — design spec (safed daag ad v2)

## Style Prompt
Bright, high-energy Indian health-ad edit modeled on the client's reference: a punchy brightened grade on every AI clip, big bold Hindi headlines that slam in on the voiceover, an effect on every shot (counters, flashes, light sweeps, blur whips, red "problem" grade), the logo on screen at all times and a brand-green claim strip along the bottom. Warm and hopeful after the turn at 9.4s.

## Colors
- Leaf green `#3E9B2F` — logo green, accents, progress lines
- Deep green `#1E5A1A` — claim strip, label pills
- White `#FFFFFF` — headlines (with heavy dark shadow)
- Turmeric `#F5B82E` — numbers (years, ₹, 2 लाख+)
- Alarm red `#D7262E` — the problem beats only (₹ counter, red grade)
- WhatsApp green `#25D366` — CTA button only

## Typography
- Mukta 800 / 700 (Devanagari + Latin) — headlines, pills, strip
- Teko 600 — big numbers

## What NOT to Do
- No burned-in text from the source clips (only clean windows or crops)
- No black frames, no black tail after the CTA
- No "48 घंटे" style result-time claims; no side-by-side before/after
- Red only in beats 3–4 (the problem); never after the turn
- Headlines never over the eyes of a face
