# DESIGN — MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV (client edit, ARV-reference style)

Source of truth for this build: `_reference/creator-library/arv-brothers/style-distilled.md` (client's reference set).
Client preference: **fast-paced**. The source is pre-edited Hindi talk + launch b-roll, so **audio is never cut**. Pace comes from a text card every ~1s, punch-in zooms inside long takes, and flash/whip accents at chapter changes.

## Style Prompt
Indian auto-creator spec reel. Huge keyword cards in ultra-condensed italic type: chrome gradients, colored glow, dark drop shadow. Hero words sit behind the host's head (person matte). Every spoken spec gets its own card on its exact word. English on-screen text over a Hindi VO.

## Colors (2 workhorse + 1 payoff)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Gold chrome headline | `#FFF6D0 → #F5C800 → #B86A00` |
| Ice cyan spec | `#39D5F5` (glow `rgba(57,213,245,.75)`) |
| Workshop red (Option C) | `#E0162B` |
| Lime payoff (price only) | `#B8FF1A` |
| Text white / shadow | `#FFFFFF` / `rgba(0,0,0,.85)` |

## Typography
- Display: **Anton** (ultra-condensed) for giant behind-head words
- Spec: **Barlow Condensed** 800 italic
- Script subline (Option B): **Kaushan Script**

## The three design options (per beat)
- **A: Behind-Head Chrome** (ref Dd6LYV2hYpr): gold-chrome giant word whip-slides in behind the head; specs in cyan with letter-scatter assemble; lime price.
- **B: Spec Pop + Script** (ref Dds-z4GhTAe): front-of-frame yellow-glow value plus a white script subline, blur-to-sharp pop; two-tone white chrome with yellow letters.
- **C: HUD / Red Workshop** (ref wa-…/DfIDBcttx84): red chrome words typed in behind the head; racing HUD stat plates (black bar + red slash); callout box with corner brackets for price.

## What NOT to Do
- No subtitles or full sentences. Keywords only, 1–3 words.
- No typos (the references have them; we don't).
- No more than 2 accent colours plus the payoff colour in any option.
- Don't cut or retime the client's audio.
- Don't leave the old burned-in captions visible. Cover them.
