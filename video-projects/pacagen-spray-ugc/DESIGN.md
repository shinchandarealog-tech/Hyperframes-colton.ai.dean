# DESIGN: Pacagen spray UGC (Meta ad)

## Style Prompt
Native, unpolished UGC. A real creator talking to camera at home, cut against phone B-roll that literally shows each line. The only added layer is small native-style captions. It must not look like an ad.

## Colors
- Captions: `#FFFFFF` for the whole video (single color, per PREFERENCES.md), soft dark shadow `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- Everything else is the footage itself, with no graphics, overlays or color grade.

## Typography
- Inter, weight 600, 54px, sentence case, 1–4 word chunks, hard swaps (no animation)

## Layout
- Meta Reels/Stories safe area on 1080×1920: text only inside x 65–1015, y 270–1250
- Captions are centered horizontally; the line sits at y≈910–975 (mid-frame, on the torso). Over bottle close-ups they move to y≈330 so the label stays clear

## What NOT to Do
- No transition effects, zoom punch-ins, shaders, stickers, logos or end card
- No music or SFX, only her voice
- No karaoke highlights, color changes, pill backgrounds, heavy stroke or emoji
- No B-roll that doesn't literally match the line it covers
