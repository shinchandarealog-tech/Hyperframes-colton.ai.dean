# DESIGN — Anatomy 4 Components Reel

## Style Prompt
@alihassan.works-style talking head: speaker full-screen, kinetic captions on the chest that blur in word by word, numbered titles, checklists. Cut away to real stock B-roll (Mixkit, free license) shown in a rounded card on a Tyrian Purple stage with Citron captions beneath. Editorial serif + clean sans, like the palette card.

## Colors
- `#66023C` Tyrian Purple: stage backgrounds, chips, pills, text shadows
- `#CAD183` Citron: caption/headline text, borders, glows, ticks
- `#ffffff` small supporting lines only

## Typography
- Instrument Serif (italic for emphasis) for display words
- Inter 600–900 for supporting lines and labels

## What NOT to Do
- No colors outside purple/citron (+white small text)
- Never cover the face during face segments (overlays stay below y≈1060 or above y≈420)
- No hard cuts into B-roll: always streak / punch / slide
- Never stretch 720p B-roll full-screen (soft); keep it in the card
