# DESIGN — MBBS First Year Books Reel

## Style Prompt
Talking-head reel in the style of @alihassan.works: the speaker stays full-screen, and the "captions" are kinetic text blocks sitting on the chest area — words blur in one by one in sync with speech, numbered lists build as glowing pills, and sub-points tick in as an animated checklist. Clean, premium, minimal.

## Colors
- `#053264` Midnight Fjord — pill/card backgrounds, bottom background wash, text shadows
- `#ccffbc` Mint Cream — all caption/headline text, pill borders, checkmarks, glows
- `#ffffff` — small supporting lines only (at 92% opacity)

Rule: navy is always the background, mint is always the text (inverted only on the final CTA pill).

## Typography
- Inter 800–900 for headlines, Inter 500–600 for supporting lines.

## What NOT to Do
- No colors outside the two-tone palette (plus white for small text).
- No bottom subtitle bar — the text blocks are the captions.
- Never cover the face (keep overlays below y≈1060).
- No hard cuts between text blocks — blur-dissolve them.
