# Udaan Cricket Academy With School — Design

## Style Prompt
Energetic sports-academy admissions reel. Real academy footage + AI cinematic clips, cut on the voice-over beats. Brand language comes straight from the Udaan logo: deep navy, cricket-ball red, clean white. Headline text sits in solid navy / red blocks with bold condensed caps — the same look as the burned-in text on the AI clips, so added text and AI text feel like one system.

## Colors
- `#14213D` — Udaan navy (text blocks, split-screen backdrop)
- `#D7192A` — cricket-ball red (accent strips, divider, highlight words)
- `#FFFFFF` — white (text on navy/red, flashes)
- `#EFEBE3` — card cream (only used to blank the AI CTA phone number)

## Typography
- Barlow Condensed 800 (headlines, all caps), 700 for secondary lines

## Rules
- Logo (transparent cutout) top-right for the entire video.
- Only headline text from the client script; never re-type text that an AI clip already shows.
- No background music. VO only. Clip audio muted.

## What NOT to Do
- No dark chrome / grid / grain motion-philosophy look — this is a bright brand piece.
- No text inside the bottom 380px (Reels UI) or under the logo.
- No phone number on screen until the client supplies the real one.
- Don't cover the speaker-less AI clips' burned-in text with new text.
