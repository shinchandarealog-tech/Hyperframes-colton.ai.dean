Virtual camera

Pick the Camera tool, then drag the preview to pan and scroll (or use Zoom below) to zoom. Each change sets a keyframe at the playhead, and the camera eases between keyframes.

Base framing per shot

Your footage is 16:9, so each camera angle gets its own 9:16 crop. The virtual camera moves on top of this.

Speed ramp

Tap the curve to add a point, drag points to shape it, double-tap a point to remove it. Up is faster, down is slow motion.

Speed changes also change your voice timing, and your profile says to keep your audio as recorded. Best on silent moments or close-ups of a watch. In the final render the audio is sped up or slowed to match, with the pitch kept.
