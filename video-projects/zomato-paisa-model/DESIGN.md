# DESIGN — zomato-paisa-model

Built "like @finish-edits" (`_reference/creator-library/finish-edits/videos/DeyegA0I0MR/analysis.md`).

## Style Prompt
Warm, cinematic podcast edit. Amber-graded talking head with crushed blacks; every sentence becomes kinetic type in the empty left half of frame — a small Inter lead-in plus a giant condensed Bebas keyword. Money/outcome words in gold gradient, one key word per line on an orange highlight box. Cut-away motion-graphic inserts (₹ hook, order cards, chalkboard money-flow, final equation) illustrate the idea being spoken. Cuts land on the word; inserts get a cyan light-streak.

## Colors
- `#140D08` — warm near-black stage (dominant)
- `#C8742E` — orange highlight box / "jaa raha" (money going out)
- `#F3D98A → #D9B25A → #8C6A2A` — gold gradient (money coming in, outcomes)
- `#FFFFFF` / `#F4EDE4` — type, chalk
- `#1F3A2E` — chalkboard green (board insert only)
- `#4FD6E0` — cyan light streak (transitions only)

## Typography
- Bebas Neue — hero keywords
- Inter 600/700 — lead-in lines
- Instrument Serif — hook only
- Caveat — chalk handwriting (board insert only)

## What NOT to Do
- No text over the speaker's face (keep it in the left ~50%)
- No bottom-of-frame karaoke subtitles
- Never more than one highlight-box word per caption group
- No flat cool/neutral footage — always the warm grade
- No Zomato logo or brand red (avoid implying an official/branded piece)
